---
name: Financial correction boundaries
description: Why permanent account identity does not authorize freezing transaction-level corrections or inferring historical ownership.
---

Permanent entity-account identity is not a policy of immutable payment
attribution or allocation destinations. Existing staff allocations and
source-proven transaction-link reconciliation are intentional capabilities.
Any new manual-payment approval/reversal policy needs separate agreement.
Uncertain historical ownership must never be auto-corrected.

**Why:** The user explicitly separated preventing ownership changes from
transaction-level corrections. Relabeling an account would reinterpret attached
history without moving it, whereas approved reconciliation has source proof for
the specific transaction links it corrects.

**How to apply:** When extending ledger safeguards or historical review, preserve
that distinction. A mismatch is a reason to investigate, not authority to choose
an owner or invent a transfer workflow.
