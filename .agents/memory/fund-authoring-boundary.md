---
name: Fund authoring boundary
description: Scope of Fund typography and palette choices versus existing content and configured Benefit Colors.
---

Fund typography and palette standardization is an authoring convenience, not an exclusive color policy, a site-theme change, or permission to restyle stored documents in bulk.

**Why:** The product request explicitly preserves deliberate/imported formatting, existing sent documents, custom colors, delivery branding, and database-backed Benefit Color references.

**How to apply:** Offer shared Fund swatches wherever a user authors a free-form color. Do not seed Benefit Colors, replace their configured-option selectors with swatches, or migrate existing content to make its appearance match new authoring defaults.
