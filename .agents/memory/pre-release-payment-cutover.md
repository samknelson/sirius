name: Pre-release online payment cutover
description: Owner-authorized disposal of legacy attempt-based online payments during consolidation.
---

The owner stated: “this is just a dev environment” and “this product has not shipped live”; payments made through the old attempt-based flow may safely be deleted, and their evidence does not need to be maintained.

**Why:** This explicitly replaces the preservation/archive requirement for the pre-release consolidation, not a general permission to delete financial data.

**How to apply:** Limit cleanup to the old online flow and its related postings. Preserve unrelated manual/imported payments. Do not apply this authorization to future production payments.
