---
name: Relationship date authority
description: Fund ruling on missing source dates and the boundary between relationships and benefit entitlement.
---

The Fund permits `2000-01-01` solely as the required-start fallback when a
source relationship start is blank. An active source relationship with no end
must remain open; missing dates do not prove that it ended. Keep genuine dates
and explicit inactivity evidence. Do not relax the required-start or overlap
contract to solve missing-source-date problems.

**Why:** A synthetic end attached to missing starts incorrectly made active
relationships appear ended and blocked November Carelon person-month groups.
The user explicitly ruled that eligibility plugins, not carrier feeds, own
benefit entitlement.

**How to apply:** Interpret source relationship dates independently of carrier
delivery needs. Do not alter benefit eligibility policy to compensate for an
import defect, and keep cleanup and bulk eligibility scans separately authorized.
