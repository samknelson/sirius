---
name: Benefit History design direction
description: User's selected direction and boundaries for a worker benefit History redesign.
---

The user selected **smart-grouped coverage eras** over a benefit-first spans view for further refinement of worker Benefit History. This is a design direction, **not approval to implement it in the production app**. Current Benefits remains the default view.

**Why:** The user wants to scan stable coverage periods without losing the ability to inspect monthly records, understand gaps and coverage-set changes, or correct an underlying entry. Benefits the member provides to dependents must be distinct from the member's own or received coverage; the existing History data does not provide that reverse relationship.

**How to apply:** Continue exploring the era concept in isolated previews until the user explicitly requests integration. Preserve expandable monthly evidence and truthful boundary explanations. Keep dependent-provided coverage in a clearly labeled separate lane rather than silently folding it into the member's coverage eras; confirm whether it should affect era boundaries before implementing that behavior.
