name: Worker coverage month relationship
description: User-confirmed clarity preference for how upcoming work months relate to later coverage months
---

For the Worker Coverage card, explain upcoming periods as **hours worked in a work month counting toward a later coverage month**. Keep hours-threshold status separate from a confirmed coverage decision; do not say future hours automatically "provide coverage."

**Why:** The user described an explicit month-to-month relationship as much clearer than the earlier future-period cards, but disliked a connector positioned between the labels rather than aligned with the months.

**How to apply:** In future iterations of this card or its production integration, keep the two months and hours values in a readable sequence, with any connector aligned to the values rather than floating between headings.
