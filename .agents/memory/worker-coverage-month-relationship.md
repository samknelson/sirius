For the Worker Coverage card, explicitly link **hours worked in a work month** to a **later coverage month**. The user chose the label “Hours grant coverage in” for that connection. Keep each future period's hours-threshold status visually and verbally separate from the confirmed current coverage decision.

**Why:** The user described an explicit month-to-month relationship as much clearer than the earlier future-period cards and requested the stronger “Hours grant coverage in” wording despite the nuance that thresholds are not decisions. After trying an arrow aligned near the value rows, they asked for a larger arrow consistently centered between the two month areas.

**How to apply:** In future iterations or production integration, keep the two months and hours values in a readable sequence, with a connector centered horizontally and vertically between the areas at the same position across rows. Preserve the distinction between a future hours signal and an actual coverage determination elsewhere in the card.
