name: Worker coverage month relationship
description: User-confirmed clarity preference for how upcoming work months relate to later coverage months
---

For the Worker Coverage card, explicitly link **hours worked in a work month** to a **later coverage month**. The user chose the label “Hours grant coverage in” for that connection. Keep each future period's hours-threshold status visually and verbally separate from the confirmed current coverage decision.

**Why:** The user described an explicit month-to-month relationship as much clearer than the earlier future-period cards, disliked a connector floating between the labels, and later requested the stronger “Hours grant coverage in” wording despite the nuance that thresholds are not decisions.

**How to apply:** In future iterations or production integration, keep the two months and hours values in a readable sequence, with any connector aligned to the values rather than floating between headings. Preserve the distinction between a future hours signal and an actual coverage determination elsewhere in the card.
