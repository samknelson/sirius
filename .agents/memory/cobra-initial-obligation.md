name: COBRA initial obligation
description: Confirmed business definition of the initial payment obligation.
---

The initial COBRA payment obligation covers every month from the coverage-start month through the election month, inclusive.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected this rule on 2026-10-15 after the lifecycle review found that the old whole-account balance calculation did not define it. Paying only the first coverage month is not sufficient for a retroactive election.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when evaluating first-payment enrollment; evaluate later monthly debt separately. Do not invent forfeiture or reinstatement policy from a missed payment deadline.
