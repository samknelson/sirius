---
name: Email consent placement
description: Owner expects email opt-in management beside the saved address, not only in communication composition.
---

Staff must be able to manage email opt-in from contact and worker Email tabs,
while retaining the existing Email Comms control.

**Why:** The owner considers the address-maintenance tab a natural place to
manage consent; staff should not need to open communication composition to do it.

**How to apply:** Share the existing email-address consent record and staff
permissions across these surfaces. Manage the saved address, not a temporarily
typed unsaved replacement, and do not introduce separate contact-level consent.

Invoice-ready notifications to a delegated payer must obey the existing email
opt-in requirement, including when the employer enters a new payer address.
Saving someone as an employer contact is not permission to email them.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose to retain opt-in rather than make payment
handoffs a transactional-email exception.

**How to apply:** Show blocked-notification feedback and let authorized staff
manage the existing consent record; never enable opt-in as a side effect of
saving or designating a payer.
