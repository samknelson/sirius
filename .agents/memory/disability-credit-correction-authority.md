---
name: Disability Credit correction authority
description: Approval, saved threshold, and annual capacity when corrections restore Disability Credit.
---

A threshold-only DC removal releases an annual month slot; it is not a reservation
for a future correction. A later downward correction may restore the approved
month only if annual capacity is still available. Administrative and case-lifecycle
removals are never an implicit authorization to restore credit.

**Why:** The approved correction scope preserves the existing annual limit and
eligibility rules. Reusing an earlier approval cannot displace months that have
since consumed the released capacity.

**How to apply:** Treat the saved continuation threshold as the correction
authority, not today's rules. Require the case to remain approved and prove the
hours-only removal; leave conflicting ownership or exhausted capacity for staff
review rather than choosing another case or over-allocating.
