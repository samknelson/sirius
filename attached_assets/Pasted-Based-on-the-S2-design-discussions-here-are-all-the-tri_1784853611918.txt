Based on the S2 design discussions, here are all the trigger events identified for rescanning benefits:

## Trigger Events for Benefit Rescan

### From the July 16 S2 design session (most detailed source):

**Scan criteria — who gets scanned (non-election-based triggers):**
- Anyone with an **active election**
- Anyone with a **benefit in the preceding month**
- Anyone with **hours impacting the calculated month**

**Data-change triggers (preferred architecture):**
- Preferred approach: trigger-based rescanning (any data change rescans affected records)
- Review queue for any trigger that removes an active benefit; auto-proceed for additions
- Elections end via a job that fires on permanent break in hours

### From the July 22 S2 session — future coverage triggers:
- Any trigger (new election, payment received) puts worker back in scan queue for the relevant month
- Specifically called out: **new election** and **payment received**

### From the WMB Event design (July 17):
- Scan starts/stops are logged as events; if a **benefit is subsequently granted for that month**, the system auto-deletes the corresponding termination event — implying benefit grants also feed back into the rescan/event logic
- COBRA queue can reference WMB termination events to check for a corresponding COBRA case — termination events act as a downstream trigger for COBRA processing

### From earlier bug/enhancement discussion (Nov 21, needs to be added to S2):
- When source employer or subscriber changes, new benefit record must start — applies to **COBRA transitions** and **dependent-to-self coverage changes**. This was flagged as a current system gap, not yet confirmed as built into the S2 trigger list.

### Automation target (not yet a live trigger):
- COBRA-to-employer transition triggers automatically when hours eligibility is restored — this is listed as an automation goal (fixing the "Jose Garnica gap"), not confirmed as implemented.

---

**Summary list as currently discussed:**
1. Active election exists
2. Benefit existed in preceding month
3. Hours change impacting the calculated month
4. New election submitted
5. Payment received
6. Permanent break in hours (ends elections)
7. Benefit source/subscriber/employer change (COBRA↔employer, dependent→self) — flagged as needed but not confirmed built
8. Hours eligibility restored → COBRA-to-employer transition (automation target, not yet live)
