# S2 Appeals Workflow — Spec v2 (post Sep 2 meeting)

## 1. Scope & architecture

- Appeals are a case type with **appeal categories**: `Benefit Appeal` (v1 build) and `Claims Appeal` (later)
  - One system tracks all appeal types (Kristen's requirement); they differ in effect: benefit appeals impact the eligibility engine and grant exemptions, claims appeals are tracking/note-taking (e.g., doula, MLK claims the fund adjudicates directly)
  - Carrier-handled claims appeals (e.g., Kaiser plan denials) stay outside the system
- Built on the grievances framework, simpler with fewer steps
- Purpose: operationalize the benefit-denial letters that aren't going out today, and memorialize verbal denials

## 2. Intake

- **Trigger: member's verbal request** — member calls an MSR (or emails Luis, etc.) asking for a benefit they're ineligible for
- MSR verbally denies, tells the member two things at once: (a) an official denial letter is coming, (b) if you disagree, send us something in writing — no need to wait for our letter
- MSR opens the appeal case on the member's behalf: selects worker, benefit requested, denial reason
- **Permissions:** any staff (per prior decision; MSRs confirmed as the front door)
- **No portal self-service in v1** — legal requires a written submission; the "tiny hurdle" is intentional. Portal-generated PDF deferred pending legal confirmation on electronic submission
- **One member letter total** — lawyers confirmed workers don't write twice; the single written letter is the trustee appeal

## 5. Actions & outputs per state

- **Submitted → Auto-Denied:** automatic on case creation; fires Lob benefit-denial letter. First appeal is *always* denied — trustee review legally requires a documented initial denial per the SPD
- **Trustee Review:** case queued for monthly trustee meeting; outcomes entered **per case** (prior decision)
- **Approved (terminal):** single action fires the blanket approval letter and auto-sets the exemption (start date) on the worker's benefit record — no manual policy change; eligibility scans check exemptions; dependents inherit subscriber exemptions
- **Closed–Denied (terminal):** auto-sends Denial of Appeal letter via Lob with SPD citations
- **Terminal means terminal:** exemptions don't expire, and revocation never reopens a case (prior decision)

## 6. Letters

1. **Benefit denial letter** — auto via Lob at case creation; official written "no" + how to appeal to trustees in writing
2. **Approval letter** — blanket template, no variable reasons
3. **Denial of appeal letter** — variable data: denial reason + SPD section citations (legally required)
