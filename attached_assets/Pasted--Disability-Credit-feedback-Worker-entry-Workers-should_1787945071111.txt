## Disability Credit feedback

- **Worker entry:** Workers should only be able to start a Disability Credit case when they have at least three months of FMLA recorded. Otherwise, direct them to contact the fund.
- **Denial-letter exception:** Members should not self-upload denial letters. Fund staff should initiate an exception case when a member believes they should qualify despite not having three FMLA months. > there is no workflow for this currently; staff need to be able to initiate a case whenever **FMLA-eligible is not met**
- **Workflow simplification:** Combine “Mark as Ready” and “Send to Queue” into one action.
- **Approval queue:** A central reviewer should periodically review and approve prepared cases before coverage months arrive.
- **Dashboard:** Rename “Active Grants” to a clearer label such as **Currently on Disability Credit**. Keep an active-case view, but simplify the FMLA-eligible display to a count/link rather than a truncated list.
- **Denial letters on dashboard:** The dedicated denial-letters section may not be necessary.

## Disability Credit Experience Spec

1. Purpose

Provide a controlled workflow for granting Disability Credit while keeping member self-service simple, staff preparation clear, and final approval centralized.

2. Member experience

- Show Disability Credit self-service only to workers with **3+ months of FMLA** in the rolling 12-month period.
- Eligible workers can upload a Disability Credit form to create a case.
- Ineligible workers cannot upload forms or denial letters.
- Show an instruction: **“You are not currently eligible to submit a Disability Credit request. If you believe you should be eligible, please contact the Fund.”**

3. Fund-initiated exception cases

- Fund staff can create a Disability Credit case for an ineligible worker when supporting documentation, such as a denial letter, must be reviewed.
- The exception pathway is staff-managed; it is not exposed as a separate member upload flow.

4. Case preparation

Staff can:

- Review uploaded files.
- Classify documents, including Disability Credit form, doctor’s note, employer accommodation letter, and denial letter.
- Upload additional documentation.
- Complete a readiness checklist:
  - Disability Credit form received
  - Doctor signature present
  - Phone number present
  - Doctor address present
  - Date present
- Select requested grant months.

5. Submission and approval

- Replace separate **Mark as Ready** and **Send to Queue** actions with one action: **Send for Approval**.
- Sending for approval validates the readiness checklist and selected months.
- Cases in the approval queue are reviewed by the designated approver.
- Approver actions: **Approve** or **Deny**.
- Approved cases move to **Currently on Disability Credit**.

6. Credit rules

- On approval, calculate Disability Credit according to the worker’s plan minimum.
- If reported employer hours are below the minimum, add only the hours needed to reach the minimum.
- If employer reporting is later corrected and provides sufficient hours:
  - Remove previously granted Disability Credit hours as needed.
  - Restore the affected Disability Credit month to the worker’s available balance.
- Employer reporting remains the source for FMLA status; employers do not participate in DC approval or document review.

7. Dashboard

Display:

- **FMLA Eligible:** count with link to the full list.
- **Approval Queue:** cases awaiting final review.
- **Currently on Disability Credit:** active approved cases.
- **Annual Maximum Reached:** workers who have used their available Disability Credit months.

Remove or defer:

- Full FMLA-eligible member list on the dashboard.
- Standalone denial-letter dashboard section.
- “Cases with Upcoming Months” unless a clearer operational use is identified.
