## t19-payments — findings and S1 cleanup

### Starting position

Six reject classes, 150 payments, none previously investigated. Payments feed the ledger and the balance-parity gate, so this was the highest-consequence loader left.

### `amount_missing` — 102

Payments with no usable amount: 90 null, 9 with no amount field row, 3 zero. **98 were void/draft/cancelled** — nothing posted, nothing to migrate.

Four were `Cleared`. Three of those had zero-for-zero or no ledger entry. **One did not:**

- **Payment 16790527** showed $0.00; its ledger entry showed **$50.00 Cleared** (Adjustment – Charge, Jesus Guzman, November Contribution).

Ledger ruled authoritative; payment corrected to $50.00 in S1. That payment now migrates.

**Left open:** 16790527 (Charge, $50, posted) and 16790533 (Credit, no ledger entry) look like an intended offsetting pair where only the charge side landed. Jesus Guzman may have been charged $50 and never credited back — a live billing question for Laura or Anna, not a migration issue.

### `payer_ref_missing` — 40 → 0

Every payment now resolves a valid payer. Cleared incidentally by the night's test-data deletions, which removed the test contacts and employers that were dangling payers.

### `account_unensured` — 3

Three adjustment credits against **retired TRFEE ledger accounts** (nids 11694615, 12315269) — accounts that should never have received payments. No ledger entries; nothing posted. Two already `Canceled`, one `Cleared` with nothing behind it. Allowed.

### `date_missing` / `payment_type_missing` — 1 each → 0

Both single records, fixed in S1.

Incidental finding: `field_sirius_datetime` is null on the vast majority of payments; the loader falls back to `datetime_created`. The single reject was the case lacking both.

## Net result

**Allow list:** `amount_missing,account_unensured` — 101 void payments and 3 retired-account adjustments, all genuinely unmigratable.

**Fixed in S1:** 6 payment records corrected, 1 erroneous ledger entry removed via the application.

## Ledger observations worth carrying

**Payment nodes and ledger entries can disagree.** 16790527 said $0.00 against a $50 ledger entry. S1 permits the two sides to diverge; S2 should prevent it structurally.

**"PAYMENTS PENDING" is being used as a payer.** A placeholder contact receiving cleared, allocated money. Whether it's recurred is unknown — a one-line query on that participant would tell you, and it's a workflow question for Laura or Anna.

**Ledger entries cascade correctly through the app.** Setting a payment to Pending removed its entry cleanly. That contrasts with the earlier finding where node deletion silently removed five test ledger rows — the app path is sound; the deletion path is not.

**Still deferred:** $187.42 of test payments in the production ledger, requiring offsetting adjustments per the append-only invariant rather than deletion.
