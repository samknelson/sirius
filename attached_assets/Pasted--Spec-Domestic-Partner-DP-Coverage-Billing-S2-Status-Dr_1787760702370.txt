# Spec: Domestic Partner (DP) Coverage & Billing — S2

**Status:** Draft for review
**Sources:** Meeting notes May–Aug 2026 (cited inline). Open questions flagged as **TBD**.

---

## 1. Overview & Goals

Automate domestic partner coverage billing, payment enforcement, and tax reporting in S2, replacing the manual S1 process. Context: DP health benefits are federally taxable income to the participant unless the DP qualifies as a tax dependent; the fund must charge monthly premiums, remit taxes quarterly, and issue W-2s annually. Population is small (~20 DPs).

**Non-goals:**
- Single-to-family tier via DP — explicitly not supported by the fund; DP-with-children deemed too edge-case for current population
- Employer-side involvement — DP account is separate from employer accounts; the employer side is irrelevant

---

## 2. Data Model

### 2.1 DP Account
- Dedicated account per subscriber for DP charges, separate from employer accounts and from COBRA and hours-purchasing accounts (per the account-separation decision: cleaner reporting, no sub-balance queries, eligibility plugin checks multiple accounts)

### 2.2 DP Rate Table
- Separate rate history table from COBRA — DP and COBRA rates change differently
- Rate = difference between subscriber's tier without DP and tier with DP added
- No "tier" field; covered-lives count computed at eligibility feed time (consistent with COBRA rate table design)
- **TBD-1:** Family-to-family-plus-DP rate — awaiting legal input; placeholder in system
- **TBD-2:** No-tier-bump edge case — if adding the DP doesn't change the covered-lives tier, is the charge zero?

### 2.3 Relationships
- **TBD-3:** No "DP's child" relationship type exists in S1; a worker→DP→child structure was floated but deferred. Given Non-goals above, likely out of scope for v1 — confirm.

---

## 3. Charge Generation

- Charges are **not** generated by the benefit scan (performance)
- **Cron job** logic:
  1. Find active elections with a DP dependent
  2. Check whether the subscriber has coverage next month
  3. If no charge exists for that month, write one to the DP account
- Charge amount derived from worker rate, benefit, and the DP rate table (parallel to COBRA charge automation design)

---

## 4. Payment & Coverage Enforcement

- Coverage is **not activated until payment is received** (COBRA-like)
- Hard cutoff model (established Aug 2026 in S1 practice, carries into S2):
  - No payment → no coverage for the following month
  - Members in arrears must pay all owed months plus the current month to restore coverage
  - No retroactive termination for months already covered
- Auto-revoke benefit if payment not received
- DP arrears affect **only the DP dependent**, never the subscriber
- DP charges are not subject to the three-month arrears rule that applies to primary members; DPs are cut off immediately on nonpayment — distinction confirmed legally defensible
- Grace period is plan-defined, expected tighter than COBRA's federally mandated timeline
  - **TBD-4:** Exact grace period length

---

## 5. Member-Facing Interface

- Worker interface to view owed/paid months and pay them
- Prepay capability

---

## 6. Tax Reporting

- Taxable-income treatment: monthly collection tracking; quarterly tax payments to government; ADP payroll tax reporting setup (Anna)
- Annual W-2 issuance; withholding components similar to payroll (FICA/Social Security/Medicare)
- Tax-dependent exemption: if the DP qualifies as the participant's tax dependent, benefits are not taxable
  - **TBD-5:** Documentation/verification workflow for proving tax dependency (likely requires proof of tax dependency status)
- All DP payments recorded under the **subscriber** account, not the dependent (process rule from W-2 review)

---

## 7. Migration (S1 → S2)

- No DP loader exists; DP records handled via **manual import**
- Pre-July-2026 DP charges were removed from S1 (charges run forward from July 1 only, no back pay) — migrated balances should reflect this baseline
